  1. Write one line.

    A passing idea, a rough note, a question you cannot answer yet. Type it the moment it arrives; the workspace is already open.

  2. Ask for the shape.

    “Turn this into a card.” “Draft a deck.” The assistant reads what you attached and writes a first version, in the open, so you can argue with it.

  3. Keep it, on purpose.

    Nothing is published until you say so. Review the diff, tag it, save it — and it joins a library that stays on your disk.

Everything you keep, in one library.

Ask for a card, an article, a deck, or a paper. Nothing is published until you save it, and everything you save is searchable, tagged, and still editable.

Your notes never leave your machine.

  • No account Nothing to sign up for, nothing to sign back in to.
  • No cloud workspace Nothing to sync and no server to trust — your notes are yours to keep.
  • No telemetry The only network calls are the model provider you configure — and a chat platform only if you connect one.
